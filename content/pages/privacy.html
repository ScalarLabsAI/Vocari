Conversations in Vocari

No Vocari account is required for a nearby session. The voice connection runs directly between nearby phones. No Wi-Fi router or internet connection is required. It does not use a Vocari cloud voice service. Vocari does not currently provide audio recording.

Optional live transcript display and explicit transcript saving are separate features. Saved or shared text needs the appropriate consent. “Private” describes the local, personal-audio design; it is not a claim of end-to-end application encryption.

This website

This version of getvocari.app has no analytics script, advertising pixel, visitor identifier or cookie-based tracking added by Vocari. GitHub Pages and other services involved in delivering the site may process connection information under their own policies.

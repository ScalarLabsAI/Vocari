## Suggested six entries

### JBL Endurance Peak 3
- Category: Sports; type: in-ear true wireless, ear hooks.
- Card copy: Secure-fit ear hooks, four beamforming microphones and an IP68 earbud rating. A candidate for active use with a sealed in-ear fit.
- Calling evidence: Official US product specifications list HFP 1.7 and hands-free calls. Maximum talk time is listed as 5.5 hours.
- Noise distinction: Microphone beamforming supports calls; Ambient Aware/TalkThru are listening features. Do not call either proof of noise-free Vocari audio or hearing protection.
- Purchase/spec source: https://www.jbl.com/ENDURANCE-PEAK-3.html
- Image status: No commercial reuse license found. Link to product gallery on the official page only.

### soundcore AeroFit 2 (A3874)
- Category: Sports; type: open-ear true wireless, adjustable ear hooks.
- Card copy: Open-ear earbuds with four-position adjustable hooks, four microphones with AI noise reduction, and an IP55 earbud rating.
- Calling evidence: Official product page explicitly documents Bluetooth 5.4 and four microphones for calls. The reviewed page did not explicitly document HFP, so record “Bluetooth calling documented; HFP not independently documented here.”
- Noise distinction: AI noise reduction is for outgoing call audio; the open-ear design leaves the ear canal open. Do not imply listening ANC.
- Purchase/spec source: https://www.soundcore.com/products/a3874-aerofit-2-open-earbuds
- FAQ source: https://service.soundcore.com/eu/article-description/soundcore-AeroFit-2-Earbuds-FAQ
- Image status: No commercial reuse license found. Link to product gallery on the official page only.

### Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen)
- Category: Personal & professional; type: in-ear true wireless.
- Card copy: Sealed in-ear earbuds with adjustable listening noise cancellation, call microphones and IPX4 water resistance. Either earbud can be used for calls.
- Calling evidence: Product page documents Bluetooth, built-in microphone, phone calls and up to four hours maximum talk time. Bose's 2025 announcement documents AI call-noise suppression with eight microphones; that is a manufacturer feature claim, not a Vocari result.
- Noise distinction: Listening ANC and outgoing call-noise suppression are separate features.
- Purchase/spec source: https://www.bose.com/p/earbuds/bose-quietcomfort-ultra-earbuds-2nd-gen/QCUE2-HEADPHONEIN.html
- Additional microphone source: https://www.bose.com/pressroom/bose-announces-the-quietcomfort-ultra-earbuds-2nd-gen
- Image status: No commercial reuse license found; express prior consent is required for commercial republication under the reviewed terms. Link to official product gallery only.

### Sony LinkBuds Open (WF-L910)
- Category: Personal & professional; type: open-ring true wireless earbuds.
- Card copy: An open-ring design, IPX4 rating and Bluetooth HFP support. A candidate for people who prefer to keep their ears open to their surroundings.
- Calling evidence: Official specifications explicitly list HFP and HSP; maximum continuous communication time is 4.5 hours.
- Noise distinction: Sony explicitly states this model does not offer active noise cancellation or traditional noise isolation. Do not import ANC claims from LinkBuds Fit or other Sony models.
- Purchase source: https://electronics.sony.com/audio/headphones/all-headphones/p/wfl910-b
- Specification source: https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/wireless-headphones-bluetooth-headphones/wf-l910/specifications
- Image status: No commercial reuse license found. Product page footer says reproduction without written permission is prohibited. Link to official gallery only.

### Shokz OpenRun Pro 2
- Category: Sports; type: open-ear wraparound headset using bone and air conduction.
- Card copy: Open-ear wraparound fit, dual microphones with AI call-noise reduction, and IP55 water resistance. Available in standard and mini sizes.
- Calling evidence: Official product/detail pages document Bluetooth 5.3 and voice calls. Exact HFP support was not independently located in reviewed official material; do not assert profile version.
- Noise distinction: Dual-microphone AI noise reduction processes outgoing voice. This is an open-ear design, not a hearing protector. Avoid repeating the manufacturer's percentage noise-reduction or cycling-speed claims as proven real-world outcomes.
- Purchase source: https://shokz.com/products/openrunpro2
- Feature source: https://shokz.com/pages/openrunpro2
- Image status: No commercial reuse license found. Link to official product gallery only.

- Suggested default filters: All candidates; Sports; Personal & professional; Enterprise & industrial; optionally style (in-ear/open-ear/boom microphone). “Tested” should show an honest zero-result state if none have evidence; it should not hide all candidates by default.

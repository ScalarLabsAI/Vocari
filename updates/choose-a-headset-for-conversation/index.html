Equipment guide

Choose a headset for conversation, not just music

Why the microphone, Bluetooth call path, listening ANC and hearing protection deserve separate checks.

By Vocari · 4 min read

Great music playback is only half of a two-way conversation. Vocari also needs the headset microphone, at the same time as the earpieces, on the phone you are actually using. That is why a music review or a gaming-mode latency claim cannot establish Vocari compatibility.

Look for a real calling path

Bluetooth’s Hands-Free Profile (HFP) provides a microphone-and-playback route for calls. A manufacturer listing Bluetooth phone calling or HFP gives us a reason to research a headset. It still does not prove that the product has passed a Vocari test.

Start with a normal call on each phone to check the physical headset. Then check a live Vocari session in both directions. Device model, system version, fit and microphone position can all affect the result.

Three different meanings of “noise”

Listening noise cancellation changes what reaches your ears. Microphone noise suppression processes the voice signal sent to the other person. Rated hearing protection is a separate protective function with specific ratings, fit requirements and use instructions.

None of these can stand in for the others. Active noise cancellation is not a hearing-protection certification. A call microphone advertised as noise-reducing does not guarantee your voice will be clear in every environment.

Choose the physical format for your task

An open-ear design leaves the ear canal open and can suit people who do not want a sealed fit. It does not guarantee environmental awareness or protect hearing. In-ear earbuds may offer a more isolated listening experience, but fit varies. A boom microphone gives you a way to position the microphone closer to your mouth.

For a setting that requires hearing protection, look at the exact protective model and follow workplace requirements. US NRR values and other rating systems are not interchangeable, and a laboratory number does not promise the reduction every wearer will achieve. Eartip or mounting variants can carry different ratings.

Read testing labels literally

Our catalog distinguishes manufacturer-documented features from Vocari evidence. “Untested with Vocari” means the product has not completed a documented evaluation for publication. It is neither a failure result nor a compatibility certification.

A useful test records the exact headset, phones, operating systems, app version, connection method and surroundings. It checks microphone and playback in both directions, delay, stability, screen-off behavior and recovery. Until that evidence is available, we do not publish performance rankings.

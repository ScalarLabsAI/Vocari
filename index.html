HOW IT WORKS

Connect. Pair. Talk.

Vocari removes the radios, button presses, and exposed speaker audio from ordinary walkie-talkie communication.

1

Connect your earbuds

Each participant connects compatible Bluetooth earbuds or a communication headset to their phone.

2

Create a Vocari link

One person creates the nearby session. The other joins using the displayed QR code or connection code.

3

Speak normally

Both people can talk and listen continuously while their phones handle the connection.
